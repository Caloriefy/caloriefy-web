Privacy Policy

Thank you for using Caloriefy AI (“we”, “our”, “us”).
Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website, mobile app, and related services (collectively, the “Service”).

By using our Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 1 November, 2025

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a better experience and improve our Service. The types of data we collect include:

  1. Personal Information: When you create an account, we may collect:

    • Name

    • Email address

    • Password (encrypted)

    • Profile information (like gender, age, height, weight, and fitness goals if you choose to share them)

  2. Nutrition & Activity Data: To help you track your progress, you may optionally log meals, calories, macronutrients, and physical activity data.

  3. Device & Usage Data: We collect data such as:

    • Device type, OS, and version

    • IP address and general location

    • App usage patterns and interactions

  4. Integrations (Optional): If you connect a third-party service (like Apple Health, Fitbit, or Google Fit), we access only the data you allow and use it strictly to enhance your experience.

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Provide and personalize your experience

  • Analyze trends and improve app performance

  • Generate AI-based meal suggestions and insights

  • Communicate updates, tips, or important notices

  • Maintain security and prevent misuse

Data Storage & Security

Your information is securely stored and encrypted in transit and at rest.
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of your data.

If you delete your account, we permanently remove or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable timeframe.

Sharing of Information

We may share your data only in the following cases:

  • With service providers: who assist in app hosting, analytics, or email delivery (all bound by confidentiality agreements).

  • For legal reasons: if required by law or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

  • With your consent: if you explicitly agree to share data with a coach, nutritionist, or other third-party apps.

Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data. You can:

  • Access, edit, or delete your information anytime.

  • Withdraw consent for data sharing.

  • Request a copy of your stored data by contacting us at privacy@caloriefy.ai

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep you signed in

  • Save preferences

  • Understand how users interact with our platform

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary to provide the Service or comply with legal obligations. If you delete your account, your data is permanently removed within 30 days.

Children’s Privacy

Our Service is not directed toward children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we learn that a child has shared personal data with us, we will delete it promptly.

Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons.
The updated version will always be available on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: privacy@caloriefy.ai
Address: J's Labs, 2416 Market Street, San Francisco
